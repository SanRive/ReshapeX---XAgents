Designing the Solution
                         Mass notification system design must take ambient noise as well
                         as sounder performance into consideration when ascertaining
                         the effective coverage area of an alarm horn. Likewise, installation
                         location and proximity to workers is vitally important for strobe lights.

3D Coverage: identify and document the optimal and safe signaling solutions.

3-dimensional signaling (HxWxD) range is determined by lab testing every
signaling device in a controlled environment, with outside ambient noise taken
into consideration.

3D-Coverage provides certainty for those involved in creating signaling systems and
safety for personnel who occupy spaces in which these systems are used.
