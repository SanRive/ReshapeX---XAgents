Alarms for Fixed Gas Detection Systems

IIAR-2A - Safe design of closed circuit ammonia refrigeration
systems require at minimum one audible and visual alarm to
be placed in the room or area around an ammonia engine or
machinery. According to IIAR, the audible alarm is required
to be at a sound pressure level (SPL) of 15 dBa (decibles)
above the average ambient sound level and 5 dBa above the
maximum sound level of the area in which it is installed. At
no point can a sound meter read lower than a 5 dBa offset at
any point within the area that the alarm is covering. We make
it easy to choose your audible and visual alarms.
