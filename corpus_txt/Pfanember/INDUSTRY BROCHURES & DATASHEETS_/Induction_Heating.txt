Induction heating is a fast and energy-efficient method
of heating electrically conductive materials. Choosing
the right cooling system is essential for an induction
process to work properly and many factors need
to be considered, including heat load, installation
environment, energy and water usage or maintenance
requirements. Pfannenberg’s chillers guarantee a central
and economical solution and perfectly meet the need
for precise temperature control and absolute reliability.
Whether it is a compact or large chiller, maximum
cooling capacity in the smallest possible footprint is
guaranteed thanks to the heavy-duty micro channel
condensers that offer high efficiency and long service life
in hot, corrosive, or dirty ambient environments.
