                                                                                   Purpose

                                                                                   Emergencies aboard boats require immediate action, whether they
                                                                                   are a man overboard, generator failure or a fire. To insure timely
                                                                                   warnings of such events, visual and audible alarms need to offer
                                                                                   superior coverage in rugged, weatherproof designs. Additionally,
                                                                                   providing unique, distinctive alarms for different events can allow
                                                                                   the crew to react more quickly and more effectively.



                                                                                   Pfannenberg’s PATROL and PYRA product lines offer you
                                                                                   modern, visual and audible alarm devices to satisfy both the
                                                                                   requirements of the American Bureau of Shipping and the
                                                                                   Marine Equipment Directive.
