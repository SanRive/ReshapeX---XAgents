Air/Air Heat Exchangers
One of the best technologies to use when the surrounding temperature is less than the target temperature is air to air technology (∆T≥10°C). An
air/air heat exchanger removes heat from inside of the enclosure to the cooler environment using the least amount of energy while still providing
a closed loop ingress protection.
