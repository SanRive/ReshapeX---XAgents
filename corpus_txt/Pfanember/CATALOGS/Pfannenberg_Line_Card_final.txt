Pfannenberg is a global manufacturer of Thermal Management, Liquid Cooling Solutions and Signaling Technologies. Established in
1954 by Otto Pfannenberg, the global headquarters is in Hamburg, Germany. The North American headquarters, established in 1997,
is in Western New York outside Buffalo, NY where our manufacturing operations are also located. Additional sales and manufacturing
locations include in Italy, the United Kingdom, Singapore, China, and Brazil.
