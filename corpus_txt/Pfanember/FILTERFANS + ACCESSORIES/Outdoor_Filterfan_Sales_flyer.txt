    RAIN, SHINE OR SNOW....WE HAVE YOU PROTECTED




For over 60 years Pfannenberg has been protecting electrical
enclosures from heat damage and ingress of liquid and solid objects.
For indoor applications our Filterfan® protects against water and
dust (NEMA 12/IP55). For extreme applications (NEMA 4/4X) the
addition of a rainhood provides weatherproof protection. The
Pfannenberg Outdoor Rated Filterfan® (NEMA 3R) is designed for
outdoor weather where resistance against falling rain, ice formation,
and UV degradation is important to ensure operation of apparatus
inside enclosure.
