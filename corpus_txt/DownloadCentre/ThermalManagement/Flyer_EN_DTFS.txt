DTFS Green Series: Product benefits at a glance


                                   Flexible mounting
                                   •    Fully integrable in the door or side
                                   •    Suitable for external mounting on the door or side installation
                                   •    Suitable for narrow enclosure designs
                                   •    Quick and easy installation: uses the same cut-out



                                   Environmentally friendly
                                   •    In accordance with the “F-Gas“ regulation:
                                        Use of R-513A, a low GWP refrigerant
                                   •    Maximum energy efficiency: EER > 2
                                   •    Low amount of refrigerant contained




  Various applications
  •   Smaller dimensions: Cooling units fit into

      planning & construction
  •   400V and 115V versions do not
      require additional space due to
      the integrated transformer

  The DTFS series is equipped with two variants of controllers - Display and LED variant. Both variants
